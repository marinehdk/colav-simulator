terrain vertex grid, normalized to uint16. Land cover drives approximate PBR
appearance. Surface textures are not Norwegian orthophotography. The underwater
floor is a synthetic visual taper to -80 m; it is not measured bathymetry and
does not replace backend ENC soundings or navigation authority.
