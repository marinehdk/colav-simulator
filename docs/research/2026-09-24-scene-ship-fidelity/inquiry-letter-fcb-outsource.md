**Schedule:** we expect delivery within **2–4 weeks** of award.

**Budget reference:** USD 2,000–8,000 for a freelance engagement; for a studio-grade engagement our ceiling is USD 15,000.

**Please quote and state:**

1. Your price for the scope above (fixed price preferred; otherwise your day rate);
2. Lead time from award to final delivery;
3. The number of revision rounds included, and the cost of additional rounds;
4. Whether you deliver **source files and working project files** (e.g., Blender/Maya, Substance), or final exports only.
